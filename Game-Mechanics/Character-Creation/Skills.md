# Skills

Toronto Fell Out's skill list, mapped to the [MAPLE-BUD](../to-be-integrated/not-SPECIAL.md) attributes. Each skill is governed by exactly one attribute; each attribute can govern multiple skills. Every entry here has been run through the verb-vs-noun audit in `Design-Philosophy.md` — each is a domain a character invests in over time, not a one-off action.

Skill caps follow the formula in `Design-Philosophy.md`: `Skill Maximum = 30 + ((Governing Stat Value − 1) × 10)`.

## Might

- **Melee Weapons** — bladed and blunt melee combat.
- **Athletics** — carry capacity, forcing stuck or jammed doors/obstacles, feats of raw strength.

## Agility

- **Guns** — handling and effectiveness with conventional firearms.
- **Sneak** — stealth and avoiding detection.
- **Sleight of Hand** — pickpocketing, planting items, subtle manual dexterity.
- **Acrobatics** — climbing, balance, vaulting, fall-damage reduction. Notably relevant given Toronto's vertical ruins (CN Tower, PATH tunnels, derelict towers).

## Perception

- **Lockpick** — picking locks.
- **Energy Weapons** — handling and effectiveness with laser-/plasma-tier weapons. These are rare, late-game-tier gear — access is meant to be earned (recovered from hard-to-reach caches, high-security pre-war sites, or similarly difficult means) rather than commonly available like Guns.
- **Insight** — reading people, detecting lies and hidden intent. Governed by Perception rather than Determination since it's about noticing, not swaying.

## Luck

No dedicated skill. Grants a flat bonus to every other skill (FNV/Inner Tepenia precedent: +⌈Luck / 2⌉ to all skills).

## Endurance

- **Survival** — foraging, cooking, general hazard-reading, scavenging quality.
- **Outdoorsman** — cold/extreme-weather resilience and wilderness travel/navigation. Distinct from Survival's foraging/scavenging focus — directly relevant to Toronto's radioactive winters and the rural/frontier fringes of the ruined GTA.
- **Unarmed** — hand-to-hand combat.

## Brains

- **Medicine** — healing, diagnosis, chem/drug knowledge.
- **Science** — hacking terminals, decoding data, understanding pre-war tech. The "know-how"/theoretical half, distinct from Utility's hands-on half.
- **Explosives** — handling, detecting, and disarming mines/traps, and understanding demolition/rigging. Relocated from Perception — the underlying domain is technical/engineering know-how (how the device works) rather than raw awareness (noticing it's there).

## Utility

- **Repair** — fixing/restoring items and structures, jury-rigging with mismatched parts.
- **Engineering** — building new gear, weapon/armor mods, and settlement construction (needed for turning ruins like abandoned Tim Hortons into outposts).
- **Chemistry** — synthesizing chems, drugs, and compounds. Distinct from Medicine's diagnostic/treatment knowledge (knowing what a chem does and how to use it) and from Engineering's gear/structure focus (making the chem itself).

## Determination

- **Speech** — persuasion and dialogue checks.
- **Barter** — trade prices and negotiation.
- **Leadership** — commanding and directing companions: squad tactics, morale, recruit-specific dialogue, companion combat effectiveness. Distinct from Speech's general persuasion and Barter's trade focus — this is the skill that actually delivers on Determination's own definition ("your effect upon your companions"), which neither Speech nor Barter covered on its own.

## Considered and Not Added

Weighed against Inner Tepenia's 26-skill MACHINE list and rejected:

- **Performance** (instrument-playing) — doesn't fit Toronto Fell Out's setting/tone.
- **Deception** and **Narrative** — both fold into **Speech** rather than becoming their own skills; a separate skill for each would be redundant given Speech's scope here.
- **Cryptography**, **Biology** — too narrow/redundant with Science and Medicine respectively.
- **Bladed/Blunt/Mechanical Weapons split** — keeping a single unified **Melee Weapons** skill instead of splitting by weapon subtype.
- **Intimidation** — considered as a Determination addition alongside/instead of Leadership, but risked feeling redundant with Speech (both are "talking your way through it," just different tone). Leadership was chosen instead since it covers ground nothing else touches.
