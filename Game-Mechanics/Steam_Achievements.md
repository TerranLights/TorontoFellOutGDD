## Design Notes & Open Questions

**Unshakeable — redesign needed**
A "never fail [stat/check] X" achievement is trivially achievable by simply building a character with a maxed relevant stat, and isn't a genuine achievement of *play* rather than *build*. This is a portable design lesson independent of which specific stat or check ends up being the target (Toronto Fell Out's own check for this hasn't been chosen yet — MAPLE-BUD has no single obvious "Nerve"-equivalent stat the way the original design note assumed). Three redesign directions worth keeping on file for whichever check gets picked:
- *Option A (Behavioral):* "Complete the game without ever fleeing from a combat encounter." Cannot be cheesed with stat investment; requires a consistent behavioral choice across every fight.
- *Option B (Threshold):* "Pass the single highest-threshold [stat] check in the game." Rewards specifically building for the hardest gate rather than having a high stat generally.
- *Option C (Comprehensive):* "Pass every available [stat] check in a single playthrough." Requires consistent investment across the whole game.
Option A is likely strongest — it changes how the player plays, not just what they build. This reasoning holds regardless of which stat or Toronto Fell Out-specific check it eventually attaches to.

**[NAME PENDING] — "You Break Too Easy"-style achievement**
Achievement confirmed in shape: receive every recruitable companion's romance signal line in a single playthrough. Name needs a decision. Options on file:

- "Wrong Build" (meta, slightly funny)

**World/exploration achievements — avoid the gameable-counter trap**
A portable lesson worth keeping regardless of which specific location or hub eventually anchors this category: a "visit/pass through [location] N times" achievement is too easily gamed by an enter/exit loop and doesn't actually reward anything meaningful. Prefer one of these shapes instead, once a real location/faction structure exists to hang it on:
- Broker a resolution between two opposing factions in a shared, contested space — genuinely hard, diplomatically meaningful.
- Have every relevant faction representative meet the player in a specific space in a single playthrough — rewards thoroughness.
- A specific quest resolution that makes a location the site of a significant convergence — tied to a particular story beat rather than a generic counter.
None of these are populated with real Toronto Fell Out content yet since no such hub/location is designed; keep this note on file for whenever one is.
