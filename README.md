> **The apocalypse hit Toronto... and the city fought back.**

A post-apocalyptic open-world RPG set in a ruined but strangely familiar 2097 Toronto. Explore the fallen CN Tower, raid the Eaton Centre ruins, barter in the Distillery District, and uncover what really happened when "Toronto Fell Out."

Inspired by the classic 3D *Fallout* games, but with a distinctly Canadian (and darkly humorous) twist.

![Banner Image](https://via.placeholder.com/1280x400/1a3c2e/ffffff?text=Toronto+Fell+Out+-+Post-Apocalyptic+Skyline)

## ✨ Features

- **Massive Open World** — From the flooded Harbourfront to the overgrown High Park, a fully explorable post-apocalyptic Greater Toronto Area
- **First & Third Person** — Seamless switching between immersive FPS and cinematic third-person
- **Deep Story & Choices** — Multiple factions, branching narratives, and memorable companions with strong opinions about poutine and survival
- **Crafting & Settlement Building** — Turn abandoned Tim Hortons into thriving outposts
- **Character Roleplay** — Full character creation with Toronto-specific backgrounds (e.g. "Former TTC Driver", "Kensington Market Witch", "Scarborough Tech Bro")
- **Dark Humor & Atmosphere** — The end of the world has never been this funny... or this bleak
- **Realistic Weather & Seasons** — Toronto’s legendary winter storms now come with radioactive snow

## 🎮 Gameplay

Classic Fallout-style real-time combat with V.A.T.S.-inspired targeting. Explore, loot, build, and decide the fate of the last remains of civilization in Canada’s biggest city.
