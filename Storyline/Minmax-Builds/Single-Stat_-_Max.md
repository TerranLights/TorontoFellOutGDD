# High MAPLE-BUD Stat Interactions in Toronto Fell Out

**Overview**
This document outlines "high-stat" interactions for the [MAPLE-BUD](../../to-be-integrated/not-SPECIAL.md) system when a stat is set to **8 or above**. These mirror the style of `Single-Stat_-_Min.md`'s low-stat interactions but emphasize power, excellence, and distinctive advantages (with occasional flavorful drawbacks like overconfidence or social friction).

High-stat interactions should feel **distinctive and empowering**, reinforcing the player's identity as exceptional in that area. They can open unique dialogue options, environmental shortcuts, combat advantages, crafting bonuses, social reactions, and quest paths.

Each stat section includes:
- A brief recap of the stat
- At least **7 different types** of unique interactions
- At least **3 sorts of immediate settings** where these interactions can occur

**Status note:** this is generic archetype flavor (Fallout-style high-stat interaction lists), not core worldbuilding — none of it is tied to a specific district, faction, or NPC, all of which remain undesigned for Toronto Fell Out. Combat interactions below assume Toronto Fell Out's real-time 1st-/3rd-person combat, with the targeting system (name TBD, V.A.T.S.-equivalent) referenced where relevant per `Design-Philosophy.md`.

## High Might (8 or above)

**Stat Recap**: Raw physical power, strength, and the ability to exert overwhelming force. Governs Melee Weapons and Athletics — carry capacity, forcing stuck doors/obstacles, and feats of raw strength.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Effortless Heavy Lifting**: Casually moves collapsed rubble, jackknifed vehicles, or fallen PATH-tunnel signage that normally requires a winch or a crew.
2. **Brute-Force Breakthroughs**: Smashes rusted-shut doors, boarded storefronts, or frozen loading-dock gates with bare hands or minimal tools.
3. **Intimidating Physical Presence**: NPCs (even armed ones) visibly hesitate or back down when the player squares up.
4. **Devastating Melee Dominance**: Delivers crushing blows that can stagger or drop heavily armored raiders in one or two hits.
5. **Massive Carrying Capacity**: Hauls enormous amounts of scavenged loot without encumbrance penalties.
6. **Structural Manipulation**: Physically holds open blast doors, bends rebar, or repositions heavy machinery others can't budge.
7. **Heroic Feats of Strength**: Performs crowd-pleasing or awe-inspiring displays of strength that earn respect or fear in a settlement.
8. **Overpowering Grapples**: Easily restrains, throws, or manhandles most human-sized opponents.

**Immediate Settings**
- Exploration & Environment: Forcing open paths, clearing collapsed overpasses, moving heavy obstacles.
- Combat & Confrontations: Melee dominance, intimidation checks, grappling enemies.
- Social & Roleplaying: Manual-labor jobs, impressing tough NPCs, leading a scavenging crew by raw presence.

## High Agility (8 or above)

**Stat Recap**: Speed, reflexes, coordination, fine motor control, stealth, and acrobatics. Governs Guns, Sneak, Sleight of Hand, and Acrobatics.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Superhuman Parkour & Mobility**: Effortlessly leaps gaps, scales derelict scaffolding, and navigates Toronto's vertical ruins (the CN Tower, PATH tunnels, collapsed high-rises) at speed.
2. **Near-Perfect Dodging**: Gracefully avoids most attacks, thrown debris, and traps with fluid real-time movement.
3. **Masterful Stealth**: Moves silently even across creaking ice or broken glass; can vanish from sight almost instantly.
4. **Precise Fine Motor Work**: Excels at lockpicking, pickpocketing, and delicate repairs under pressure.
5. **Lightning Reflexes**: Reacts instantly to ambushes, collapsing structures, or sudden falls.
6. **Acrobatic Combat**: Performs vaults, slides, and evasive maneuvers mid-firefight, especially effective alongside the targeting system (name TBD).
7. **High-Speed Traversal**: Crosses long stretches of the ruined GTA far faster than an average survivor.
8. **Graceful Social Movement**: Slips through crowded, tense settlement spaces with effortless poise.

**Immediate Settings**
- Exploration & Movement: Rooftops, collapsed towers, tight PATH-tunnel crawlspaces, long-distance travel.
- Combat & Action: Dodging, stealth approaches, acrobatic fighting.
- Social & Stealth: Infiltrations, black-market dealings, evasion sequences.

## High Perception (8 or above)

**Stat Recap**: Awareness, precision aim, and reading people. Governs Lockpick, Energy Weapons, and Insight.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Instant Pattern Recognition**: Notices structural weak points, ambush setups, or trap tells others completely miss.
2. **Expert Lockpicking**: Bypasses complex pre-war locks and security mechanisms with ease.
3. **Predictive Dialogue**: Anticipates what NPCs are about to say or do, enabling sharper responses.
4. **Rare-Tech Aptitude**: Handles recovered energy weapons — rare, late-game-tier gear — with unusual effectiveness for their access tier.
5. **Long-Range Precision**: Exceptional accuracy at range, compounding well with the targeting system (name TBD).
6. **Reading People**: Detects lies and hidden intent (Insight) with near-unerring accuracy.
7. **Environmental Threat-Spotting**: Notices radiation hotspots, thin ice, or unstable structures before they become a problem.
8. **Social Deduction**: Reads micro-expressions and hidden motives with surgical precision.

**Immediate Settings**
- Dialogue & Social: Persuasion through observation, reading intentions, catching NPCs in lies.
- Exploration & Puzzles: Finding hidden caches, disarming or bypassing security, spotting environmental hazards.
- Combat: Precision shooting, ambush detection, exploiting enemy weak points.

## High Luck (8 or above)

**Stat Recap**: No dedicated skill — Luck instead grants a flat bonus (+⌈Luck / 2⌉) to every other skill, per `Skills.md`. High Luck reads as a character who is simply, inexplicably favored by circumstance.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Broad Competence**: Noticeably better than expected at nearly everything, without a clear specialization — the flat bonus to all skills shows up everywhere at once.
2. **Fortunate Timing**: Consistently arrives a beat before disaster or a beat after danger has passed — a floor gives way just after they've crossed it, not while.
3. **Improbable Loot**: Finds unusually good scavenge in picked-over locations other survivors already stripped clean.
4. **Critical Moments Favor Them**: Combat and skill attempts cluster toward favorable outcomes at the margins, without ever feeling like a guaranteed win (per `Design-Philosophy.md`'s no-dice-roll rule, this is skill-threshold-adjacent flavor, not a hidden RNG system).
5. **NPCs Comment on It**: Companions and settlement NPCs notice and remark on the player's uncanny knack for landing on their feet.
6. **Unlikely Survivals**: Walks away from situations — a firefight, a structural collapse, a bad trade — that by all reasonable expectation shouldn't have gone their way.
7. **Everything Compounds**: Because the bonus is universal rather than domain-specific, a high-Luck build makes every other stat investment slightly better, rewarding broad builds over narrow ones.

**Immediate Settings**
- Any skill check, anywhere: the flat bonus applies universally rather than to a specific setting.
- Social: NPCs remarking on the player's reputation for good fortune.
- Loot & Exploration: better-than-expected scavenge outcomes.

## High Endurance (8 or above)

**Stat Recap**: Toughness, cold/hazard resilience, and hand-to-hand survivability. Governs Survival, Outdoorsman, and Unarmed.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Radioactive-Winter Resilience**: Shrugs off Toronto's irradiated cold and extreme-weather exposure that would fell a lesser survivor.
2. **Near-Endless Stamina**: Marches, fights, or works for extremely long stretches with minimal fatigue.
3. **Damage Sponge**: Absorbs hits — from weather, radiation, or fists — that would down most people.
4. **Rapid Recovery**: Recovers from exhaustion or injury faster than average.
5. **Foraging & Survival Mastery**: Finds food and reads environmental hazards where others find nothing.
6. **Unarmed Dominance**: Devastatingly effective in hand-to-hand combat, no weapon required.
7. **Outdoorsman Navigation**: Reads Toronto's ruined, snow-choked geography and frontier fringes with total confidence.
8. **Endurance-Based Leadership**: Sets an example by simply outlasting everyone else in a crisis.

**Immediate Settings**
- Exploration & Survival: Long winter treks, frontier travel, foraging.
- Combat & Survival: Prolonged fights, unarmed combat, absorbing punishment.
- Crafting & Repair: Long, grueling expeditions and marathon salvage runs.

## High Brains (8 or above)

**Stat Recap**: Intelligence, technical know-how, and demolitions theory. Governs Medicine, Science, and Explosives.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Diagnostic Mastery**: Reads injuries, illnesses, and chem interactions with expert precision.
2. **Terminal & Tech Fluency**: Hacks pre-war terminals and decodes corrupted data with ease.
3. **Explosives Expertise**: Detects, disarms, and rigs demolitions with total confidence.
4. **Technical Brilliance**: Understands and explains pre-war schematics or automated-defense systems intuitively.
5. **Complex Problem Solving**: Solves intricate mechanical or electronic puzzles others can't crack.
6. **Efficient Field Medicine**: Stabilizes companions and self with minimal resources.
7. **Pattern Recognition in Data**: Notices connections in logs, records, and terminal entries others miss.
8. **Technical Condescension (drawback)**: Occasionally comes across as talking down to less technically-minded NPCs.

**Immediate Settings**
- Exploration & Puzzles: Hacking terminals, disarming traps, decoding pre-war tech.
- Combat: Explosives handling, understanding automated-defense behavior.
- Social: Explaining technical matters, occasionally alienating less technical NPCs.

## High Utility (8 or above)

**Stat Recap**: Hands-on crafting, repair, and jury-rigging. Governs Repair, Engineering, and Chemistry.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Jury-Rig Virtuoso**: Fixes and improves gear out of mismatched, half-ruined parts that shouldn't work together.
2. **Structure to Settlement**: Turns abandoned ruins — a gutted Tim Hortons, a derelict PATH storefront — into functioning outposts.
3. **Weapon & Armor Modding**: Builds mods that meaningfully outperform stock gear.
4. **Chem Synthesis Mastery**: Cooks reliable, high-quality chems and compounds from scavenged precursors.
5. **Field-Expedient Repairs**: Fixes broken equipment mid-crisis with whatever is on hand.
6. **Salvage Efficiency**: Extracts more usable material from wrecks and ruins than an average scavenger would.
7. **Reverse-Engineering**: Figures out how a piece of pre-war tech works well enough to rebuild or replicate it.
8. **Overbuilding (drawback)**: Occasionally over-engineers a simple fix, taking longer than necessary to get something "right."

**Immediate Settings**
- Crafting & Repair: Weapon/armor mods, settlement construction, chem synthesis.
- Exploration: Salvaging ruins, rigging shortcuts.
- Combat prep: Field repairs, mod installation between engagements.

## High Determination (8 or above)

**Stat Recap**: The player's effect on companions. Governs Speech, Barter, and Leadership.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Powerful Persuasion**: Talks down hostile NPCs or rallies wary ones with unusual ease.
2. **Strong Companion Loyalty**: Both the human and non-human companion slot (see `Companion-System.md`) perform better and open up more under this player's leadership.
3. **Superior Trade Terms**: Consistently negotiates better prices, compounding with any reputation-based merchant discount already earned (see `Reputation-System.md`).
4. **Squad Tactics**: Directs companions effectively in combat, drawing out their full combat potential.
5. **De-escalation Mastery**: Talks down tense standoffs that would otherwise turn violent.
6. **Charismatic Leadership**: Naturally draws survivors together at a settlement or outpost.
7. **Reading Companion Morale**: Notices when a companion is struggling before it becomes a problem.
8. **Overconfidence (drawback)**: Occasionally oversells a plan to companions that doesn't fully pan out.

**Immediate Settings**
- Dialogue & Social: Persuasion, negotiation, faction relations.
- Companion Moments: Leadership, morale, combat direction.
- Trade: Barter outcomes at settlements and traveling merchants.

## General Notes

- High-stat interactions should feel **distinct and empowering** while sometimes carrying minor social or narrative drawbacks (e.g., High Might can intimidate would-be allies; High Brains can make others feel talked down to).
- These can trigger special dialogue flags, unique skill-check outcomes, environmental shortcuts, and quest opportunities.
- Combining multiple high stats creates strong, flavorful playstyles — see `Storyline/Minmax-Builds/README.md` for the tentative three-stat combinatorial build-archetype concept this connects to.
- Low-stat (`Single-Stat_-_Min.md`) and high-stat interactions together create strong character identity and replayability.
