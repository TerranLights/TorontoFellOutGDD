# Low MAPLE-BUD Stat Interactions in Toronto Fell Out

**Overview**
This document outlines "low-stat" interactions for the [MAPLE-BUD](../../to-be-integrated/not-SPECIAL.md) system when a stat is set to **3 or below**. These are inspired by classic Fallout low-Intelligence dialogue and events, expanded across all eight MAPLE-BUD stats.

Low-stat interactions should feel flavorful, occasionally humorous, occasionally tragic or frustrating, and mechanically relevant (affecting dialogue, skill checks, environmental interactions, combat, crafting, social dynamics, etc.). They help reinforce character identity and can open (or close) unique questlines, reputation paths, or roleplaying opportunities.

Each stat section includes:
- A brief recap of the stat
- At least **7 different types** of unique interactions
- At least **3 sorts of immediate settings** where these interactions can occur

**Status note:** this is generic archetype flavor (Fallout-style low-stat interaction lists), not core worldbuilding — none of it is tied to a specific district, faction, or NPC, all of which remain undesigned for Toronto Fell Out.

---

## Low Might (3 or below)

**Stat Recap**: Raw physical power, strength, and the ability to exert force. Governs Melee Weapons and Athletics.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Physical Inadequacy**: Cannot lift or move heavy debris, jammed doors, or supply crates that average survivors handle easily. Must ask for help or find slower workarounds.
2. **Being Physically Overpowered**: NPCs, even unarmed ones, can easily restrain, push, or manhandle the player. Raiders treat the player as non-threatening.
3. **Poor Melee Performance**: Struggles significantly in melee combat; a bat or pipe wrench feels "too heavy," and swings land weak or slow.
4. **Failure to Break or Force**: Cannot smash rusted-shut doors or weak walls that stronger characters can brute-force. Must rely on tools or lockpicking.
5. **Low Carrying Capacity & Encumbrance**: Becomes overburdened extremely quickly. Even a modest scavenging haul forces the player to drop items or move very slowly.
6. **Social Mockery or Pity**: NPCs comment on the player's weakness ("You couldn't lift a Timmies tray, could you?"). Can trigger bullying, pity, or exclusion from labor jobs.
7. **Environmental Failures**: Cannot climb certain ledges, push stalled vehicles, or hold open heavy doors. Often requires companions or gadgets.
8. **Intimidation Backfire**: Attempts to intimidate through size/strength are laughed off or provoke stronger opponents.

**Immediate Settings**
- **Exploration & Environment**: Lifting debris, climbing, forcing doors, carrying loot through collapsed ruins.
- **Combat & Confrontations**: Melee fights, being grappled or overpowered by enemies, failed intimidation checks.
- **Social & Roleplaying**: Manual-labor jobs, being mocked at a settlement bar, exclusion from "tough guy" crews.

## Low Agility (3 or below)

**Stat Recap**: Speed, reflexes, coordination, and fine motor control. Governs Guns, Sneak, Sleight of Hand, and Acrobatics.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Frequent Tripping & Falling**: Trips over rubble, ice patches, or own feet. Can cause minor damage or alert enemies.
2. **Poor Dodging & Evasion**: Struggles to avoid attacks, traps, or thrown debris. Often gets hit even when trying to dodge.
3. **Clumsy Fine Motor Tasks**: Fumbles tools, drops items, fails delicate actions like lockpicking or precise repairs.
4. **Bad Stealth & Sneaking**: Makes noise easily, bumps into things, or moves too slowly to stay hidden.
5. **Slow Reaction Time**: Takes longer to react to sudden events (collapsing floors, ambushes, gunfire).
6. **Difficulty with Acrobatics**: Cannot vault gaps, climb scaffolding, or navigate Toronto's vertical ruins that others manage easily.
7. **Weapon & Tool Mishandling**: Drops weapons mid-fight, fumbles reloads, or slips on ice mid-combat.
8. **Coordination Failures in Group Actions**: Struggles to keep pace with agile companions or perform coordinated tasks.

**Immediate Settings**
- **Exploration & Movement**: Navigating ruins, rooftops, PATH tunnels, or icy terrain; avoiding environmental hazards.
- **Combat & Action**: Dodging attacks, stealth approaches, quick reactions during fights.
- **Crafting & Fine Work**: Lockpicking, delicate repairs, handling fragile pre-war tech or traps.

## Low Perception (3 or below)

**Stat Recap**: Awareness, precision, and reading people. Governs Lockpick, Energy Weapons, and Insight.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Missing Obvious Clues**: Walks past important items, notes, or environmental tells that others notice immediately.
2. **Poor Lockpicking**: Fumbles even simple locks; breaks picks trying.
3. **Bad at Searching**: Misses hidden caches, compartments, or secret passages in plain sight.
4. **Terrible Aim at Range**: Struggles badly with anything beyond point-blank, energy weapons especially so given their rarity and precision.
5. **Difficulty Reading Situations**: Misses social cues, lies, or hidden tension in conversations (Insight failures).
6. **Overlooking Environmental Danger**: Fails to notice radiation hotspots, thin ice, or unstable structures before it's too late.
7. **Easily Fooled by Misdirection**: Falls for obvious deception or red herrings.
8. **Information Blindness**: Either misses key details entirely or gets overwhelmed trying to process too much at once.

**Immediate Settings**
- **Exploration & Investigation**: Searching rooms, examining scenes, finding hidden paths.
- **Dialogue & Interrogation**: Reading lies, noticing inconsistencies, gathering intel from NPCs.
- **Combat & Survival**: Spotting ambushes, noticing traps, aiming at range.

## Low Luck (3 or below)

**Stat Recap**: No dedicated skill — Luck instead grants a flat bonus (+⌈Luck / 2⌉) to every other skill, per `Skills.md`. Low Luck means this bonus is minimal to nonexistent, and the character reads as someone circumstance never quite favors.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Everything's Slightly Harder**: No universal bonus means every skill check sits at its raw value — nothing to fall back on at the margins.
2. **Bad Timing**: Consistently arrives a beat too early or too late — the floor gives way exactly as they cross it, not before or after.
3. **Mediocre Loot**: Picked-over locations stay picked-over; nothing extra turns up.
4. **NPCs Notice**: Companions and settlement NPCs comment on the player's rotten luck, sometimes sympathetically, sometimes as a running joke.
5. **Bad Breaks Compound**: A run of minor misfortune (a jammed gun, a missed step, a bad trade) tends to cluster rather than spread out.
6. **No Safety Net**: Because the bonus is universal rather than domain-specific, its absence is felt everywhere at once rather than in one obvious spot.
7. **Superstition Hooks**: Opens dialogue opportunities with NPCs who are superstitious about bad luck, for better or worse.

**Immediate Settings**
- Any skill check, anywhere: the absence of the flat bonus applies universally rather than to a specific setting.
- Social: NPCs remarking on the player's reputation for bad luck.
- Loot & Exploration: unremarkable scavenge outcomes, occasional bad-break events.

## Low Endurance (3 or below)

**Stat Recap**: Toughness, cold/hazard resilience, and hand-to-hand survivability. Governs Survival, Outdoorsman, and Unarmed.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Quick Fatigue**: Tires out extremely fast during physical activity or prolonged travel.
2. **Radioactive-Winter Vulnerability**: Suffers badly from Toronto's cold and radiation exposure where tougher survivors shrug it off.
3. **Frequent Illness/Injury**: More prone to sickness, frostbite, or lingering wounds.
4. **Poor Unarmed Combat**: Weak, ineffective hand-to-hand fighting; needs a weapon to be reliable.
5. **Bad Foraging**: Struggles to identify safe food or read environmental hazards.
6. **Slow Recovery**: Takes longer to heal from exhaustion or injury.
7. **Frontier Fragility**: Cannot handle extended wilderness travel or Toronto's rural/frontier fringes without heavy support.
8. **Needs Frequent Rest**: Cannot sustain long expeditions or marathon combat without breaks.

**Immediate Settings**
- **Exploration & Travel**: Long winter treks, frontier journeys, foraging attempts.
- **Combat & Survival**: Prolonged fights, unarmed combat, absorbing punishment.
- **Crafting & Repair**: Extended salvage runs or repair sessions requiring stamina.

## Low Brains (3 or below)

**Stat Recap**: Intelligence, technical know-how, and demolitions theory. Governs Medicine, Science, and Explosives.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Misunderstanding Instructions**: Frequently misinterprets orders, repair manuals, or technical explanations. NPCs have to repeat things slowly.
2. **Poor Diagnosis**: Misjudges injuries, chem interactions, or illness; bad field medicine calls.
3. **Difficulty with Tech & Systems**: Struggles to operate pre-war terminals or understand automated-defense systems.
4. **Explosives Mishaps**: Badly fumbles disarming or rigging explosives — genuinely dangerous at this stat range.
5. **Social Naivety**: Easily tricked, manipulated, or talked into bad deals; gullible in conversations.
6. **Being Talked Down To**: NPCs (especially high-Brains ones) treat the player like a child. Can trigger condescending dialogue.
7. **Memory & Planning Issues**: Forgets important details or misses obvious connections in logs and notes.
8. **Dumb-Luck Solutions**: Occasionally blunders into a correct answer through sheer persistence rather than understanding — a distinct flavor from actually knowing what's going on.

**Immediate Settings**
- **Dialogue & Social**: Being manipulated, receiving condescending explanations, failing logic-based persuasion.
- **Exploration & Puzzles**: Failing to solve terminal puzzles or decode data, missing obvious clues.
- **Crafting & Tech**: Operating machinery, disarming explosives, understanding schematics.

## Low Utility (3 or below)

**Stat Recap**: Hands-on crafting, repair, and jury-rigging. Governs Repair, Engineering, and Chemistry.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Can't Fix Anything Properly**: Repairs hold together badly, if at all — jury-rigging tends to actually rig itself wrong.
2. **Botched Mods**: Weapon/armor modifications frequently underperform or fail outright.
3. **Bad Chemistry**: Cooked chems come out weak, inconsistent, or occasionally harmful.
4. **Settlement Construction Failures**: Struggles to turn a ruin into a functional outpost; structures are shoddy.
5. **Wastes Materials**: Uses more scavenged parts than necessary for a worse result.
6. **Reliance on Others**: Needs a companion or NPC vendor for anything beyond the most basic fix.
7. **Overcorrects with Force (crosses with low Brains)**: Tries to fix mechanical problems by hitting them, rarely well.
8. **Slow Work**: Simple repairs take far longer than they should.

**Immediate Settings**
- **Crafting & Repair**: Weapon/armor mods, settlement construction, chem synthesis attempts.
- **Exploration**: Failed salvage attempts, botched shortcuts.
- **Combat prep**: Unreliable field repairs between engagements.

## Low Determination (3 or below)

**Stat Recap**: The player's effect on companions. Governs Speech, Barter, and Leadership.

**Interaction Types**
1. **Weak Companion Influence**: Companions are harder to direct in combat and less responsive to leadership.
2. **Poor Trade Terms**: Consistently negotiates worse prices; barter checks fail more often.
3. **Companion Morale Issues**: Companions open up less, question the player more, and may consider leaving.
4. **Bad at Persuasion**: Struggles to talk down hostile NPCs or de-escalate tense standoffs.
5. **Social Isolation**: Struggles to build trust at settlements; harder to recruit companions in the first place.
6. **Failure in Emotional Appeals**: Cannot calm angry NPCs or appeal to shared values effectively.
7. **Undermines Own Plans**: Occasionally fails to sell even a good plan to a companion, causing hesitation at a bad moment.
8. **Awkward Leadership**: Struggles to rally survivors at an outpost, even with good intentions.

**Immediate Settings**
- **Dialogue & Social Encounters**: Persuasion checks, companion conversations, faction/settlement negotiations.
- **Companion Moments**: Combat direction, morale, recruitment.
- **Trade**: Barter outcomes at settlements and traveling merchants.

## General Notes

- Low-stat interactions should feel **distinct per stat** while still allowing creative overlap when multiple stats are low.
- These can be used for dialogue flags, skill-check outcomes, environmental interactions, companion reactions, and quest design.
- Consider combining low stats for even more flavorful (and punishing) moments (e.g., low Determination + low Endurance = a survivor nobody trusts to hold up under pressure).
- See `Single-Stat_-_Max.md` for the high-stat counterpart, and `Storyline/Minmax-Builds/README.md` for how these connect to the tentative multi-stat build-archetype concept.
