## What's Actually Open for Toronto Fell Out

Per `Reference/Open-Worldbuilding-Decisions.md`, Toronto Fell Out's currency question is still genuinely unresolved:

- **A default/universal currency name** (the equivalent of Fallout: New Vegas's "bottle caps") — not yet decided.
- **A tentative secondary/institutional currency** — "Canadian Tire money" has been floated as a tentative pick, positioned as the equivalent of FNV's "NCR dollars" (a specific faction or region's more official currency, distinct from the universal default). Not yet confirmed.

Both names are needed before the currency-related perks (`Fortune Finder`, `Scrounger`) in `Game-Mechanics/Perks/Perks.md` can be updated to use Toronto Fell Out's own terms instead of FNV's.

**This file does not attempt to resolve either name.** See `Reference/Open-Worldbuilding-Decisions.md` for the authoritative, up-to-date status of this open question, and update `Perks.md` directly (removing the corresponding row from that reference file) once a decision is made.
