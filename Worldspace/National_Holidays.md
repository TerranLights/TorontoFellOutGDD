# Toronto Fell Out — National Holidays

**Status: undesigned, not urgent.** Inner Tepenia's version of this document built a four-category holiday framework (civic/national, "persisted aesthetic" epochs, internationally-transcendent holidays, and celestial/faction-specific observances) whose organizing principle was thin even in its own context, and Fallout as a franchise already has its own holiday precedent (e.g., Fallout 3/4's approach to Christmas, Halloween-adjacent content) that would be a more natural reference point than reusing Inner Tepenia's own structure wholesale.

No holiday content has been designed for Toronto Fell Out, and none is invented here.

## A Starting Direction, If and When This Gets Designed

If/when holidays are designed for Toronto Fell Out, a reasonable direction — consistent with the game's own established dark-humor, Canadian-specific tone (see `README.md` and `General-Overview-Notes/Design-Philosophy.md`) — is Fallout's own well-established "the apocalypse changed how people mark time" flavor: real Canadian holidays warped, degraded, or reinterpreted by eighty years of post-collapse Toronto life. This is a nod toward a direction, not a design — no specific holidays have been named or designed.

This is low priority relative to Toronto Fell Out's more foundational open work (districts, factions, companions, the main storyline's open questions).
