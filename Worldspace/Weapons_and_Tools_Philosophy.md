# Weapons & Tools Philosophy

**Status: not yet written.** Inner Tepenia's version of this document was built around two premises that don't transfer: a nonexistent TV tie-in project depicting a pre-war civilian era, and a taxonomy of robot-maintenance tools (coolant-tending, Arcanet/electrical maintenance, fabrication-chamber upkeep) being weaponized by war — none of which has a Toronto Fell Out equivalent. There is no reusable structure here beyond the general idea that weapons should have a civilian-tool origin story, which is worth restating on its own terms rather than inheriting Inner Tepenia's specific categories.

No weapon categories, tool taxonomies, or specific weapon philosophy have been designed for Toronto Fell Out, and none is invented here.

## A Starting Seed, If and When This Gets Designed

Fallout as a franchise already has a strong precedent worth leaning on directly: ordinary civilian tools weaponized by necessity during and after a war (a lawnmower blade turned into a melee weapon, a kitchen knife pressed into service, a length of pipe). Toronto Fell Out's own Canadian setting suggests an obvious, distinctly local extension of that same idea — hockey gear (sticks, skates, goalie equipment, padding repurposed as improvised armor) as a starting reference point for the kind of "everyday Canadian object weaponized by the apocalypse" logic this game's tone already leans into (see `README.md`'s "dark Canadian twist" framing).

This is a nod toward a direction, not a design — no specific weapon categories, stats, or damage types have been created.
