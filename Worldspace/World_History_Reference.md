# World History Reference

**Toronto Fell Out is part of the shared Tepenian Universe** — the same cross-media continuity as Inner Tepenia, the "Southern Lights" TV series, the Cryptograph Helix novels, and the Outer Tepenia trilogy. The authoritative shared timeline lives in a separate repo: `TepenianUniverseTimeline` (locally at `media/Reference/TepenianUniverseTimeline`).

**Confirmed by the user (2026-07-29): Toronto Fell Out sits at a very, very early point on that shared timeline.** Concretely, per the timeline repo's own era breakdown, 2097 (Toronto Fell Out's setting) falls within **The First Interwar Period** (Sept. 26, 2083 – June 21, 2564) — the era that opens with the first global nuclear war and closes with the exile of robots to Antarctica (the Falkland Treaty). Toronto Fell Out's setting is only **14 years** after that era's opening event.

**Practical consequence:** almost none of Inner Tepenia's specific established lore exists yet at this point in the shared timeline — no sentient robots at large in society, no Antarctic exile, no Concordia, no Falkland Treaty, no Long Night War, no Zodiac districts, no Arcanet/siligel. All of that is centuries downstream. This confirms and matches the aggressive content-stripping already applied when adapting Inner Tepenia's copy-pasted material elsewhere in this repo — it's not an oversight, it's timeline-accurate.

**Confirmed by the user (2026-07-29): Toronto Fell Out is the shared timeline's "Great Lakes DMZ."** The timeline repo's own reference map (`Reference/Falkland_Treaty/Real_World_Influences.md`, cross-checking `North America with tentative labels.jpg`) already shows a post-2083 North American successor-polity roster that includes a **"DMZ strip near the Great Lakes"** — explicitly not a nation, no signatory status, previously undeveloped narrative territory. Toronto Fell Out *is* that DMZ. The game takes place shortly after the September 26, 2083 global nuclear war, within the shared timeline's "Set-Up" era (~2088–2131) — "the Fell Out" is Toronto's own regional fate within that same global war, not a separate, later, unrelated catastrophe. While the rest of the continent spent this era forming successor nations (Quebec, the People's Democratic Republic of New England, the Midwest Republic, and others per the confirmed roster), Toronto never got claimed or rebuilt — it stayed a quarantined, unclaimed ruin, which is exactly why a perimeter is still being enforced 14 years later.

**The Falkland Treaty (2564) and everything downstream of it is not relevant to Toronto Fell Out.** That's roughly 500 years in this game's future — Antarctic exile, sentient robots at large in society, Concordia, the Long Night War, Zodiac districts, Arcanet/siligel — none of it exists yet, and none of it needs reconciling against anything in this game.

**Confirmed consequence:** because this is a real post-nuclear-war setting within the shared timeline rather than a self-contained fictional wasteland, Fallout's own radiation-era material — ghouls, radiation resistance/exposure, and the radiation-themed perks/traits already in `Game-Mechanics/Perks/Perks.md` and `Game-Mechanics/Character-Creation/Traits.md` — is canonically accurate to Toronto Fell Out's actual setting, not borrowed flavor.

**The "almost" caveat still holds for everything past the DMZ/Set-Up-era connection above:** the user was explicit that "almost everything" from the wider Tepenian Universe doesn't apply yet — not "nothing," but also not "everything downstream is fair game either." The DMZ/Set-Up placement is now confirmed; don't extend further connections (specific successor-nation politics, specific later-era events) without asking.

For Toronto Fell Out's own pre-war and collapse history in more narrative depth, the closest existing material is `Reference/Main-Storyline-Candidates.md`, which lays out the CN Tower/radio "false victory" hook, the confirmed quarantine-perimeter-as-map-boundary mechanic, and the still-open questions (which bordering successor polity is enforcing the perimeter, what's buried under the city, the automated defense system's original purpose, and who the frequency-squatting faction turns out to be).
