# Skills

Toronto Fell Out's skill list, mapped to the [MAPLE-BUD](../to-be-integrated/not-SPECIAL.md) attributes. Each skill is governed by exactly one attribute; each attribute can govern multiple skills. Every entry here has been run through the verb-vs-noun audit in `Design-Philosophy.md` — each is a domain a character invests in over time, not a one-off action.

Skill caps follow the formula in `Design-Philosophy.md`: `Skill Maximum = 30 + ((Governing Stat Value − 1) × 10)`.

## Might

- **Melee Weapons** — bladed and blunt melee combat.
- **Athletics** — carry capacity, forcing stuck or jammed doors/obstacles, feats of raw strength.

## Agility

- **Guns** — handling and effectiveness with conventional firearms.
- **Sneak** — stealth and avoiding detection.
- **Sleight of Hand** — pickpocketing, planting items, subtle manual dexterity.
- **Acrobatics** — climbing, balance, vaulting, fall-damage reduction. Notably relevant given Toronto's vertical ruins (CN Tower, PATH tunnels, derelict towers).

## Perception

- **Explosives** — handling, detecting, and disarming mines/traps.
- **Lockpick** — picking locks.
- **Energy Weapons** — handling and effectiveness with laser-/plasma-tier weapons. These are rare, late-game-tier gear — access is meant to be earned (recovered from hard-to-reach caches, high-security pre-war sites, or similarly difficult means) rather than commonly available like Guns.
- **Insight** — reading people, detecting lies and hidden intent. Governed by Perception rather than Determination since it's about noticing, not swaying.

## Endurance

- **Survival** — foraging, cooking, resisting environmental hazards (radiation, Toronto's radioactive winters).
- **Unarmed** — hand-to-hand combat.

## Brains

- **Medicine** — healing, diagnosis, chem/drug knowledge.
- **Science** — hacking terminals, decoding data, understanding pre-war tech. The "know-how"/theoretical half, distinct from Utility's hands-on half.

## Utility

- **Repair** — fixing/restoring items and structures, jury-rigging with mismatched parts.
- **Engineering** — building new gear, weapon/armor mods, and settlement construction (needed for turning ruins like abandoned Tim Hortons into outposts).

## Determination

- **Speech** — persuasion and dialogue checks.
- **Barter** — trade prices and negotiation.

## Luck

No dedicated skill. Grants a flat bonus to every other skill (FNV/Inner Tepenia precedent: +⌈Luck / 2⌉ to all skills).
