# Fallout: New Vegas — Skills (Reference List)

Reference material for skill design. Maps each SPECIAL attribute to the skills it governs in *Fallout: New Vegas*.

## Strength
- Melee Weapons *(proxy: carry weight)*

## Perception
- Energy Weapons
- Explosives
- Lockpick

## Endurance
- Survival
- Unarmed

## Charisma
- Barter
- Speech

## Intelligence
- Medicine
- Repair
- Science

## Agility
- Guns
- Sneak

## Luck
No specific associations — instead grants +n to every skill, where n = ⌈Luck / 2⌉ (Luck ÷ 2, rounded up).
