# MAPLE-BUD (Toronto Fell Out's Attribute System)

Toronto Fell Out's answer to Fallout's SPECIAL system, split into two words: **MAPLE** and **BUD**.

## MAPLE

- **M**ight
- **A**gility
- **P**erception
- **L**uck
- **E**ndurance

## BUD

- **B**rains *(i.e., "Intelligence")*
- **U**tility *(i.e., your knack for crafting, repairing, and jury-rigging gear out of whatever survived the apocalypse)*
- **D**etermination *(i.e., your effect upon your companions)*
